About me

My fascination with robotics started early: I was the kid who took apart every toy to understand what was inside. That curiosity still drives me today as I complete my B.Eng. in Robotics Engineering at Université de Sherbrooke.

Today I focus on autonomy: state estimation, planning and control in ROS 2, deployed on embedded computers like the Jetson Orin Nano and validated in the field: on a race track, an airport apron and a factory floor.
